About

My name, Ai-nur, means “Moon-Light Gift” in Kazakh.

I am a mechanical engineer specializing in scaling high-throughput laboratories, integrating automation, and optimizing workflows to accelerate science in biotech and pharma. My work bridges engineering, automation, and design.

Beyond engineering, I care about protecting the Caspian Sea's endangered biodiversity and supporting underrepresented communities in STEM. Whether longboarding, writing poetry, or painting, I see creativity as the spark for meaningful change.

Twenty-eight generations by the Caspian Sea

In Kazakh culture it is essential to remember at least seven generations of ancestors. Thanks to my grandfather, a history professor, our family preserved the names of all 28 generations. The genealogy was written in 1946 by Sultan Rsaluly, chronicler of the Zhenet division, and supplemented in 1960 with the accounts of my great-grandfather Nygmet Imanuly.
