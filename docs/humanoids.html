Humanoid Robots for the Lab

What

I am building humanoid robotics systems for laboratory environments. The approach starts with human teleoperation to perform real-world laboratory tasks and generate high-quality datasets that can be used to develop increasingly autonomous robotic capabilities.

Why

Scientists spend a significant portion of their time on repetitive, manual, and sometimes hazardous tasks. Humanoid robots can operate within existing laboratory infrastructure, improving safety, reducing operational bottlenecks, and letting researchers focus on higher-value scientific work.
